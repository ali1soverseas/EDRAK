## 1. Hybrid Monetization Model
Starting in 2026, GitLab transitioned from fixed per-seat add-on SKUs (legacy GitLab Duo Pro and Duo Enterprise) to a hybrid monetization model combining base seat subscriptions with consumption-based **GitLab Credits**.

## 2. GitLab Credits Architecture
- **Unit Economics**: GitLab Credits are priced at approximately **$1.00 USD per credit** on demand.
- **Bundled Monthly Allowances**:
  - **GitLab Premium** ($29/user/month): Includes Duo Core features (Code Suggestions and basic Chat) plus a promotional bundled credit allowance of **$12 in GitLab Credits per user per month**.
  - **GitLab Ultimate** (Enterprise Tier): Includes full security/compliance capabilities plus a promotional bundled credit allowance of **$24 in GitLab Credits per user per month**.
  - **Free Tier**: Free tier organizations can now purchase GitLab Credits pools directly to consume agentic workflows without requiring immediate seat tier upgrades (capped at $25,000/month pool).
- **Flex Commitment Pools**: Large enterprise customers commit to annual consumption pools with volume discounts, eliminating friction for unpredictable AI workload bursts across developer teams.
