## Azure DevOps

Azure DevOps is a comprehensive platform that supports automotive compliance workflows by enabling users to customize process templates and maintain real-time traceability matrices. It integrates with various compliance software and tools, facilitating the management of regulatory standards and internal policies. The platform features a tiered pricing structure and offers robust security measures, including OAuth scopes and secure secrets management. Azure DevOps is widely adopted across sectors, including automotive, and has recently introduced updates to enhance governance and compliance reporting capabilities.

**R1. Current capabilities of Azure DevOps in supporting automotive compliance workflows.**  
_fulfilled | generally_available | confidence: 0.770 | evidence: third_party_only_

Azure DevOps supports automotive compliance workflows by allowing users to extend process templates with custom work item types, such as 'System Requirement' and 'Safety Goal'. This functionality enables teams to enforce state transitions and maintain real-time traceability matrices, facilitating ASPICE compliance for large Tier-1 suppliers. Additionally, it integrates with compliance software to manage regulatory standards, automate audits, and track documentation, reducing non-compliance risks.
