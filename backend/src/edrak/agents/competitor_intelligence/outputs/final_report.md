## Azure DevOps

Azure DevOps is a comprehensive platform that supports automotive compliance workflows by enabling users to customize process templates and maintain real-time traceability matrices, which are essential for ASPICE compliance. It integrates seamlessly with various tools, including IBM DOORS and Jira, facilitating collaboration across different platforms. The platform offers a tiered pricing structure starting at $45 per user, with additional costs for extra services, and provides extensive support options, including built-in compliance management tools. Recent updates have enhanced its governance and security features, making it well-suited for the automotive industry, particularly as vehicles become more software-defined.

_fulfilled | generally_available | confidence: 0.770 | evidence: third_party_only_

Azure DevOps supports automotive compliance workflows by allowing users to extend process templates with custom work item types such as 'System Requirement' and 'Safety Goal'. Additionally, over 68% of automotive companies have reported improved deployment efficiency after implementing DevOps practices, highlighting the platform's relevance as vehicles become more software-defined. Azure DevOps is preferred by US enterprises due to its strong integration with the Microsoft ecosystem, providing a comprehensive toolkit for compliance.
